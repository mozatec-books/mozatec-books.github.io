Card 2: Karkab's Knee

Book 1 · Chapter 3 · Self-control

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

The clue

Karkab is 5 sizes big, and he shrinks one size with every slow breath. How many slow breaths until he is size 0?

Grown-up: show the answer

5 breaths. Count them together out loud, then go back to the book.

Ask your child

  1. Where in your body do you feel angry? Hot face, tight fists, a loud tummy?
  2. What is one thing you can do before you shout?
  3. What would you say to Karkab, if he was angry?

Hot to calm game (30 seconds)

Squeeze your fists tight like Karkab. Count 3. Now open your hands slowly and breathe out like blowing a candle. Do it 3 times. Ask: which one felt calmer?

Tip

Name the feeling first ("You feel angry"). Then breathe together. Then let your child choose what to do next.
