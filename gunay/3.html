Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

The clue

Karkab locked a message in a box. Use the symbol key to read it.

⭐ 🌙 🍃 🔑

Grown-up: show the answer

WAIT. Karkab's secret: when you wait, he gets small. Find the word WAIT in the letter grid in the book.

Ask your child

  1. When is it hard for you to wait?
  2. What does calm feel like in your body?
  3. What can you do while you wait?

Tip

Practice a 10-second wait together with a slow count, then praise the trying, not only the result.
