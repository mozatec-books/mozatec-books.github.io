Card 5: The Brave Feelings

Book 2 · Chapter 3 · Karkab on the path

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

Feel it first

Fear lives in the body before it lives in words. Ask your child to show you where it sits right now: tummy? chest? fists?

The game

The 30-second hot-to-brave game. Lie on your bellies like cobras. Breathe in for 4 counts and lift your chests. Hiss out for 6 counts: "Sssss!" Three times. Then check: did the hot feeling shrink, like Karkab?

Ask your child

  1. Show me with your hands: how big is the fear now? And after the hisses?
  2. What is one brave thing you once did while you were scared?
  3. Where does Karkab sit after Günay breathes slowly?

Tip

Name the feeling to tame it. "I feel scared" is a brave sentence.
