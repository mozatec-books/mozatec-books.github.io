Card 11: The Kind Feelings

Book 4 · Chapter 3 · at the Shell Shore

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

Feel it first

Kindness has a body feeling too: a warm chest, soft shoulders, a slower breath. Ask your child where they feel it when someone is kind to them.

The game

The 30-second humming-bee game. Sit back to back. Breathe in through the nose, and hum out together: "Mmmmmm." Five times. Feel the buzz in your backs where you touch.

Ask your child

  1. Where in your body do you feel it when someone is kind to you?
  2. Why did Tilda the turtle come out of her shell?
  3. Who could you hum a calm song with this week?

Tip

Hum together in the car or before sleep. Shared humming settles two nervous systems at once.
