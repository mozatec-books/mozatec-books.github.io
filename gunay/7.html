Card 7: The Shining Door

Book 3 · Chapter 1 · before Butterfly Pose

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

The clue

The teal door has a word on it. The word has 5 letters. It is what your eyes and your mind do when they stay on one thing. Günay keeps it on one quiet star.

_ _ _ _ _

Hint: it starts with F.

Grown-up: show the answer

FOCUS. Write it on the line in the book and turn the page.

Ask your child

  1. What do you notice when you watch one thing for ten slow breaths?
  2. Why did the butterfly finally land on the flower?
  3. Can you sit like a butterfly and flap your wings slowly, ten times?

Tip

Make a "one thing" game: pick one sound in the room and listen to only that sound for 30 seconds together.
