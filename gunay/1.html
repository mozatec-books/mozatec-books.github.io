Card 1: The Door Riddle

Book 1 · Chapter 1 · before Tree Pose

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

The clue

The green door has a word on it. The word has 6 letters. It is something you do with your nose and your mouth when you are calm. Günay does it before every pose.

_ _ _ _ _ _

Hint: it starts with B.

Grown-up: show the answer

BREATH. Write it on the line in the book and turn the page.

Ask your child

  1. What do you notice when you breathe in slowly? Where does your tummy go?
  2. Why do you think Günay wobbled when she hurried?
  3. Can you stand on one foot like a tree and count to 5?

Tip

Put a soft toy on your child's tummy. Ask them to make it rise on the way in and sink on the way out.
