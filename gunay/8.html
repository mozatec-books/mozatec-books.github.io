Card 8: The Busy Mind

Book 3 · Chapter 3 · on the Starfall Sea

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

Feel it first

Busy thoughts are like falling stars: there are too many to catch, and that is okay. Ask your child what the loudest thought in their head is right now.

The game

The 30-second float-the-boat game. Sit like little boats: feet lifted, arms forward. Breathe in "I float", breathe out "like a boat". Five times. Wobbling is allowed. Coming back is the exercise.

Ask your child

  1. What is the loudest thought in your head right now? Can you wave at it and let it float by?
  2. How did Nova catch her star in the end?
  3. What helps you come back when your mind wanders?

Tip

When the mind wanders, say "come back" kindly — never angrily. Coming back kindly is the whole practice.
