Card 4: The Brave Door

Book 2 · Chapter 1 · before Lion Pose

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

The clue

The red door has a word on it. The word has 4 letters. It is what a lion does to let the fear out. Günay does it and the door bursts into butterflies.

_ _ _ _

Hint: it starts with R.

Grown-up: show the answer

ROAR. Write it on the line in the book and turn the page.

Ask your child

  1. What does your body do when you feel scared? Where do you feel it?
  2. Why do you think Ember could not roar at first?
  3. Can you roar like a lion — first loudly, then softly, then in a whisper?

Tip

Try three lion breaths before bed: breathe in through the nose, roar it out through the mouth. Each roar softer than the last.
