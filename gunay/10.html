Card 10: The Kind Door

Book 4 · Chapter 1 · before Flower Pose

Grown-up, this page is for you. Read the clue aloud to your child. Let them think. When they answer, go back to the book and turn the page.

The clue

The rose door has a word on it. The word has 4 letters. It is what you are when you help someone, share something, or say sorry first. Günay learns that it opens more doors than any key.

_ _ _ _

Hint: it starts with K.

Grown-up: show the answer

KIND. Write it on the line in the book and turn the page.

Ask your child

  1. What do you notice in your chest when you open your arms wide like a flower?
  2. Why did the gray bud not bloom when Günay shouted at it?
  3. Can you bloom like a flower for three slow breaths?

Tip

Kindness starts inside: before a hard talk, put a hand on your child's heart and yours, and take one breath together.
